The 4 rules we use
Constant Rule

A plain number becomes 0.

5 → 0
Power Rule

Bring the exponent down in front, then subtract 1 on the exponent.

x³ → 3x²
Constant Multiple Rule

Multiply the exponent by the number in front, then subtract 1 on the exponent.

4x² → 4 × 2x = 8x
Sum / Difference Rule

Work on each piece by itself. Keep the + and − signs.

x² + 3x → 2x + 3
